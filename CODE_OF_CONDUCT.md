# Code of Conduct

WebFixBench contributors must keep discussion respectful, specific and focused
on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct
and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable in project spaces.

Raise conduct concerns privately with the maintainer through the contact method
on <https://alexsinyaev.com/>. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, limit participation,
or ban participants when needed to protect the community.

Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome. Critique evidence, methods and
code; do not attack people.
