# Project identity and affiliations

WebFixBench is an independent open-source research and developer-tooling project
for reproducible evaluation of LLM-assisted code review.

It is **not a separate incorporated company or legal entity**.

## How to describe WebFixBench

For websites, conference material, grant or startup applications, and other
general-purpose forms, the preferred short description is:

> WebFixBench is an independent open-source benchmark for measuring the
> reliability of LLM-assisted code review on web-application changes.

When a form asks for the **project, product, startup or organization name**,
use:

> WebFixBench

When a form explicitly asks for a **legal entity, registered company name,
incorporation number or incorporation date**, do not present WebFixBench as a
separate corporation. Use the legally applicable details of the operator and
describe WebFixBench as the project where the form allows clarification.

When a research program requires an **academic or institutional affiliation**,
the relevant research participant should use their real institution and
institutional email. The project name remains WebFixBench.

## Research claims

Project identity and organizational wording must not be used to overstate the
scientific status of the benchmark.

In particular:

- v0.1 is a release candidate and no real-model results are published yet;
- the current suite contains sixteen synthetic cases;
- fifteen labels are frozen after maintainer review and one remains pending;
- inter-rater agreement and independent academic per-case review are not yet
  measured;
- WebFixBench does not certify a model or product as safe.

See [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md), [DATASET.md](DATASET.md) and
[ETHICS.md](ETHICS.md) for the research boundaries.
